# Tableau-Arthritis-Visualization

For this project, I had to Use the "Chronic Disease Indicators Data" dataset to create a dashboard of CDC data that includes at least three different visualizations. It is worth noting that, to use the visualization, one has to download the data from https://data.cdc.gov/Chronic-Disease-Indicators/U-S-Chronic-Disease-Indicators/hksd-2xuw/about_data from October 16 2024.

I decided to make my visualizations about arthritis levels in adult men and women. The main visual would be a hex map that shows the arthritis levels in the U.S. states, as well as a few other locations. The two other visuals would compare arthritis levels between adult  men and women. 
I started the visualizations by making my hex map. I made a map that had all of the states and a few other locations before making it in the hex format. I then set it up to have all of the varying levels of arthritis. The arthritis rate was displayed by how blue each respective location was. I then made sure the locations were spaced out well and moved on to the other two visuals. One of the other visuals was a bar chart with men and women as the two respective bars. The other was a timeline comparing men and women’s rates of arthritis over time. 

I attempted to use this visual to describe the prevalence of arthritis when looked at through metrics such as geographic location and gender. While there was a slight correlation between location and rate of arthritis in the middle of the country, there was still no major correlation based on location. This might be misleading at first as one would find it difficult to compare arthritis rates just by color and would likely have to look at the tooltip. I also found that women had more arthritis than men. The tooltip allows the viewer to discern between the arthritis rates in different locations and the overall line in the timeline allows for a good way to show the middle ground between men and women.
